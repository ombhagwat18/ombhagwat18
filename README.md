<h2 align="center">🚀 About Me</h2>

<img align="right" src="./assets/astro-removebg-preview.png" width="280" alt="Developer Illustration"/>

**Om**, here — a final-year Robotics & Automation Engineering student at K.K. Wagh Institute of Engineering, Nashik.

I enjoy building scalable, production-ready systems that connect AI perception to physical machines — vision models that catch defects in milliseconds, robot arms that plan their own trajectories, and PLC logic that turns a decision into a pneumatic actuation.

Currently, I'm deepening my work in **ROS 2 Humble, MoveIt2, YOLOv8 edge inference, and Modbus/SCADA integration**, while sharpening my understanding of real-world industrial control systems.

My goal is simple: write clean code, build reliable robots, and grow into an engineer who ships systems that work on the factory floor, not just in simulation.
