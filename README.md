<h2 align="center">🚀 About Me</h2>

<img align="right" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/VYRXfjcnW8ULe8Fq3q/giphy.gif" width="260" alt="Robot arm illustration"/>

**Om**, here — a final-year Robotics & Automation Engineering student at K.K. Wagh Institute, Nashik.

I build the layer that connects AI perception to physical machines: vision models that catch defects in milliseconds, robot arms that plan their own trajectories, and PLC logic that turns a decision into a pneumatic actuation.

Currently deepening my work in **ROS 2 Humble, MoveIt2, YOLOv8 edge inference, and Modbus/SCADA integration**, while pushing my BottleGuardPro inspection system and a Yaskawa GP12 pick-and-place cell from prototype to production-grade.

My goal: become the engineer who ships robots that work on the factory floor, not just in simulation.
